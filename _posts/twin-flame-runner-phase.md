When your twin flame pulls away, the instinct is to chase. To explain. To fix. To send the message you have drafted fourteen times.

I want you to stop.

The runner phase is not a sign that the connection is broken. It is a sign that it is working — that the mirror is doing exactly what it was designed to do, and your counterpart is not yet equipped to look at what they see.

## What the runner is actually feeling

In every reading I have done on a runner — and I have done thousands — the pattern is the same. They are not indifferent. They are overwhelmed.

The intensity of the twin flame bond activates every unhealed wound they carry, and the ego's response is flight. They feel the pull just as strongly as you do. That is precisely why they run.

## What this phase requires from you

This is the chapter where your inner work determines the timeline. Not theirs. Yours. The runners return when they are ready — and they become ready faster when the chaser stops chasing and starts healing.

Your energy is not invisible to them. Even in silence. Especially in silence.

## What I see in readings

When I scan a runner's energy, I look at three things: the degree of ego resistance, the presence of third-party interference, and the timing window. All three are readable. All three have answers.

If you are in this phase right now and you need to know where your person actually stands — that is exactly what a live session is for.
