Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Psychic Belle
Summary

This policy explains what personal data Psychic Belle collects, why it is collected, how it is used, and your rights. By using this website or purchasing any service, you agree to the collection and use of your data as described here. This includes the possibility that anonymised session data may be used for AI research and development purposes, as described in Section 6.

01

Who We Are

This website is operated by Psychic Belle, an independent psychic advisor operating internationally. References to "we," "us," or "Belle" in this policy refer to Psychic Belle as the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

For any privacy-related enquiries, contact: support@psychicbelle.com

02

03

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Delivering live and offline psychic reading sessions you have purchased
  • Managing your Sanctuary member account via Clerk
  • Processing payments and managing credit balances via Stripe
  • Sending your reading responses by email
  • Maintaining session continuity across visits via browser localStorage
  • Preventing fraud and enforcing our Terms & Session Agreement
  • Improving the quality of readings and services
  • AI model research and development — see Section 6

We do not use your data for third-party advertising. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

04

Third-Party Data Processors

We work with the following trusted third-party processors who may handle your personal data on our behalf. Each operates under its own privacy policy and data processing agreement:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Handles card data under PCI DSS compliance. stripe.com/privacy
  • Clerk — authentication and account management. GDPR-compliant. clerk.com/privacy
  • Tidio — live chat platform. Stores chat transcripts for session continuity and quality assurance. tidio.com/privacy-policy
  • Netlify — website hosting and form submissions. GDPR-compliant. netlify.com/privacy
  • Google Fonts — font delivery. May process IP address for font serving.

All third-party processors are contractually required to process your data only as directed by us and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

05

Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses browser localStorage — not traditional cookies — to store your session state locally on your device. The following values are stored:

  • b_secs — your remaining session credits in seconds
  • b_pkg — the package you purchased
  • b_started — whether your session has been started
  • b_expiry — the expiry date of your credits (12 months from purchase)
  • b_terms_ok — whether you have accepted the Terms & Session Agreement

This data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or site data.

Third-party services (Clerk, Tidio, Stripe) may set their own cookies for authentication, session management, and fraud prevention. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

06

AI Research & Model Development

Important disclosure: Psychic Belle is engaged in the development of AI-powered spiritual guidance technology. Anonymised, non-identifying conversational data collected through this platform may be used to train, fine-tune, and improve AI models as part of this research. By using this service, you consent to this use of your data as described below.

The following types of data may be used for AI research and model development:

  • General age ranges derived from sessions (e.g. "late 20s", "mid 40s") — never exact dates of birth
  • The nature and category of issues brought to sessions (e.g. twin flame separation, no contact, person of interest, reunion) — without identifying the specific individuals involved
  • How specific types of issues were explored and resolved within sessions — conversational patterns and guidance approaches only
  • The frequency and types of questions asked across sessions — aggregated thematic data only
  • Energetic and astrological themes that arose during readings — without linking to any individual

What is never used for AI training:

  • Names — yours or anyone else's
  • Exact dates of birth
  • Email addresses or any contact information
  • Location data of any kind
  • Any information that could identify you or another person

All data used for AI training is fully anonymised and aggregated before use. The resulting AI models are developed exclusively for use within Psychic Belle's own platform and services and will not be shared with or sold to third parties.

The legal basis for this processing is legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in developing proprietary AI-powered guidance technology, balanced against the strong anonymisation protections described above.

Right to opt out: You may opt out of your session data being used for AI research at any time by emailing support@psychicbelle.com with the subject line "AI Data Opt Out". We will process your request within 30 days. Opting out has no effect on your ability to use the service or receive readings.

07

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data — retained for the duration of your active account. Deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request
  • Session and reading data — retained for up to 3 years for quality assurance and AI research purposes, after which it is anonymised or deleted
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations
  • Chat transcripts — retained by Tidio per their data retention policy; Belle retains anonymised extracts for up to 3 years
  • LocalStorage data — stored on your device only; session credits expire after 12 months
08

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Payment data handled exclusively by Stripe under PCI DSS compliance
  • Authentication managed by Clerk with industry-standard security practices
  • No storage of financial details on our own servers

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to affect your rights and freedoms, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

09

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data where there is no lawful reason to retain it
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including AI model training (see Section 6)
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior lawful processing

California residents (CCPA): You have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@psychicbelle.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10

Children's Privacy

This service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data through this website, please contact us immediately at support@psychicbelle.com and we will delete it promptly.
